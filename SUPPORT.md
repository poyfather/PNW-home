# Support

## Something is broken

Open a **Bug report** issue: https://github.com/poyfather/PNW-home/issues/new/choose. Include your TV model, Tizen version, and what you saw.

## It installs but does not look right on my TV

Open a **Model report** issue. Only one TV (a 2018 UN65NU8000) has been tested, so reports from other models are the most valuable thing you can send.

## How do I...

Check the README first: configuration, adding photos, desktop preview and the remote's controls are all there. For setup questions (Developer Mode, Tizen CLI, certificates) see [docs/INSTALL.md](docs/INSTALL.md). If you are still stuck, open a **Feature request** or **Bug report** issue and say it is a question.

## Security

See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Please do not report security problems in public issues.
